# 📌 Introduction to GIS & Remote Sensing

Geographic Information Systems (GIS) and Remote Sensing (RS) are closely related fields that work together to capture, store, analyze, and visualize spatial data.

## 💡 Core Concepts

*   **GIS**: A framework for gathering, managing, and analyzing data. It analyzes spatial location and organizes layers of information into visualizations using maps and 3D scenes.
*   **Remote Sensing**: The process of detecting and monitoring the physical characteristics of an area by measuring its reflected and emitted radiation at a distance (typically from satellite or aircraft).
*   **The Synergy**: Remote sensing provides the raw data (imagery), while GIS provides the tools to process, analyze, and interpret that data in a geographic context.
