# 🛤️ GIS & Spatial Data Science Roadmaps
Whether you are a beginner or looking to advance your GIS career, follow these structured paths.

---

## 🏗️ The Foundations (Level 1)
1. **Introduction to GIS**: Understanding layers, projections, and coordinate systems.
2. **Software Mastery**: Learn **QGIS** (Open Source) or **ArcGIS Pro** (Commercial).
3. **Cartography**: Basics of map design and visualization.

## 🐍 Geospatial Programming (Level 2)
1. **Python Basics**: Variables, loops, and functions.
2. **Spatial Libraries**: `Geopandas`, `Shapely`, `Fiona`, and `PyProj`.
3. **Database Management**: Learn **PostgreSQL** with the **PostGIS** extension.

## 🚀 Advanced Spatial Data Science (Level 3)
1. **Remote Sensing**: Satellite imagery processing with `Rasterio` or `SentinelHub`.
2. **Spatial Statistics**: Spatial autocorrelation and regression models.
3. **Machine Learning**: Random Forest, CNNs, and Object Detection for imagery.
